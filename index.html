For My Love 💜

A little piece of my heart, made just for you.
✦ This flower blooms only for you ✦

My love,

I’m writing this because sometimes words feel too small to say everything I really feel, but I need you to know, you’re the first thing I think of when I wake up and the last thing on my mind before I sleep. Even on the quiet days, even when we’re apart, even when everything feels heavy… you’re still there, in every part of me.

Thank you for staying. Thank you for choosing me even when I’m hard to understand, even when I overthink too much, even when I’m not my best self. You make me feel safe in a way I never thought I could be, like I don’t have to hide anything from you. With you, I can just be me.

I know I’m not perfect. I get scared, I worry too much, I have days where I feel lost and I don’t know how to say it. But please believe me when I tell you, my heart is yours. Every bit of it. No matter how far apart we are, no matter how busy life gets, I’m always yours. I see a future with you. I want to be the one who holds your hand through everything, the one you come to when you’re happy and when you’re tired, the one who never makes you feel like you’re alone.

You’re beautiful, not just to look at, but the way you care, the way you laugh, the way you just are. You make everything brighter just by being in it. I’m so proud of you, for how hard you try, for how much you give, for the amazing person you are. Don’t ever forget how special you are to me.

I promise I’ll keep trying. I’ll keep learning how to love you better, how to listen more, how to be the man you deserve. Whatever happens, whatever we go through, I’m not going anywhere. You’re my person. You’re the one I want.

I love you more than I can ever say.

Yours, always 💌

💜
